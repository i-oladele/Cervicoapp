Section 3.9: four sections A–D, 36 items. Section B mixes choice, multiple-response, Yes/No and True/False questions, rather than 12 Yes/No questions. Section D contains eight custom usability and cultural appropriateness statements, not the published uMARS instrument. Preserve the submitted questionnaire wording; do not substitute uMARS without researcher approval.
Scoring (decided): Knowledge is scored on the 12 items of Section B (Q11–22), 1 point each, total 0–12, using the researcher's rules: Q11 'Yes' = 1; Q12 at least one source ticked = 1; Q15 'All women' or 'All sexually active women' = 1; every other item has one correct answer. Each item's point is stored with the assessment (knowledgeScoringVersion appendix-II-12-item-v1). Assessments submitted before this change were scored on 9 items; their full answers are stored and can be rescored.
